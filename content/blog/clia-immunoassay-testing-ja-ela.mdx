## What a CLIA immunoassay analyzer actually does

Chemiluminescence immunoassay is a highly sensitive method for measuring substances that circulate in your blood at very low concentrations — hormones, vitamins and proteins. The test uses antibodies that bind to the target molecule and emit a measurable flash of light; the brighter the signal, the higher the level. This is the same technology used by large hospital laboratories, which is why it is the standard for tests where small differences in the result change the diagnosis (for example, thyroid function).
