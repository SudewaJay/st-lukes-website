Getting a blood test shouldn't cost you half a day. If you're in **[Negombo](/locations/negombo)** or **[Seeduwa](/locations/seeduwa)**, St. Luke's Medical Laboratory can come to **your home, guesthouse or office**, collect the sample, and send your report back digitally — usually **within 24 hours**. This guide explains exactly how that works, who it suits, and what it costs.

## First, an honest word about where we are

Our processing laboratory is in **[Ja-Ela](/locations/ja-ela)**, on Old Negombo Road. **Seeduwa** is about 5 km up the corridor; **Negombo** is about 15 km along the coast. We **don't run a walk-in branch** in either town — instead we serve them as **scheduled home-visit areas**. A qualified phlebotomist travels to you, and your sample comes back to our lab for processing. You get the same tests, the same equipment and the same **transparent LKR pricing** as anyone walking into our HQ.

## Who home collection is ideal for

- **Busy families and working people** who can't take time off to queue.
- **Elderly or bed-bound patients** who find travelling to a lab difficult.
- **Expats and visitors** in Negombo — we collect at your **hotel or guesthouse** and email a report you can share with a doctor **anywhere in the world**.
- **Shift workers** near the airport in Seeduwa — we schedule around early mornings and evenings.
- **Anyone on regular monitoring** — diabetes, cholesterol, kidney or thyroid — who needs repeat tests on a schedule.

## How it works — five simple steps

1. **Call [071 123 1954](tel:+94711231954)** and tell us which tests you need (or read out what your doctor wrote).
2. We confirm the **price in LKR** up front and book a **home-visit slot** that suits you.
3. A qualified phlebotomist arrives at your **home, hotel or office** and collects the sample under proper conditions.
4. The sample is transported to our **Ja-Ela lab** and run on our analysers.
5. Your **secure digital report** is delivered — usually **within 24 hours** — ready to share with any doctor, in Sri Lanka or abroad.

## What common tests cost

A few of the tests people request most often, with prices from our [full price list](/price-list):

- **Full Blood Count (FBC)** — LKR 400
- **Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS)** — LKR 280
- **HbA1c (3-month sugar average)** — LKR 2,100
- **Lipid Profile (cholesterol)** — LKR 1,500
- **Liver Function Test (LFT)** — LKR 2,250
- **Serum Creatinine (kidney)** — LKR 650
- **TSH (thyroid)** — LKR 1,800
- **Dengue NS1 Antigen** — LKR 1,200

Prefer a full check-up? Our [health packages](/packages) bundle the common tests at a set price. Every individual price is on the [price list](/price-list) — nothing is hidden.

## A quick fasting tip

Some tests — **Fasting Blood Sugar** and the **Lipid Profile** — need **8–12 hours of fasting** (water is fine). For those, we book an **early-morning slot** so you can eat straight after. When you call, tell us the tests and we'll advise exactly how to prepare.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do you have a lab in Negombo or Seeduwa?**
No — our lab is in Ja-Ela. We serve both towns as **home-visit areas**: we come to you, so you don't need to travel to a lab.

**Can you collect from my hotel in Negombo?**
Yes. We regularly visit homes, guesthouses and hotels along the **Negombo beach and Ettukala strip**. We collect at your room and email your report.

**Will I get my report by email?**
Yes — reports are delivered as **secure digital files**, usually within 24 hours, which is convenient if you want to forward results to a doctor overseas.

**How much extra is the home visit?**
You pay the same published **LKR price per test**, plus a standard home-visit arrangement our team confirms when you book. No surprises.

**Can you do regular monitoring visits in Seeduwa?**
Yes. For diabetes, cholesterol, kidney or thyroid monitoring we schedule **repeat home visits** and keep your reporting consistent.
