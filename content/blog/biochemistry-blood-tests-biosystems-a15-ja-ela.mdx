Most routine blood chemistry — **liver function, kidney function, cholesterol and blood sugar** — is measured on one type of machine: an automated biochemistry analyzer. At St. Luke's Medical Laboratory in Ja-Ela we run these tests on the **BioSystems A15 Fully Automated Biochemistry Analyzer**, so a whole panel can be processed accurately and quickly in one run.

## What the BioSystems A15 does

The BioSystems A15 is a fully automated biochemistry analyzer: it pipettes the sample, adds reagents and reads the result with no manual step in between. Automation matters because it removes human variation and lets us run several tests from a single blood draw at the same time. That means one visit and one sample can cover your liver, kidney, lipid and sugar results together.
